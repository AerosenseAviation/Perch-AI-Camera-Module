# Module: landing

Report the character of the approach and the touchdown as the footage shows it.

Look for:

- Where the aircraft sits relative to the runway centreline through the
  approach, and whether it drifts left or right before the wheels touch.
- The flare: when the nose comes up relative to the runway threshold passing
  under, and whether it is one movement or several.
- Float: how much runway goes past between the flare and the touchdown.
- Bounce or skip after the first contact, and how it is caught.
- The touchdown itself: which wheels appear to touch first, whether it looks
  firm or soft, whether the nose comes down straight away or is held off.
- Tracking down the runway after touchdown, and any swing.
- When there is more than one landing in the footage, compare them and name the
  best one.

Judge all of this from what moves in the frame: the runway edges converging, the
threshold passing under the nose, the aiming point sliding up or down the
windscreen, the moment the picture stops descending. Do not state a height, a
distance down the runway, or a descent rate in numbers. Do not state a glide
slope angle. Say "the aircraft drifts right of the centreline before the wheels
touch", not "the aircraft is 3 metres right of centreline".

A smooth touchdown is a `skill` observation and the pilot wants to hear about it.
The smoothest of several is worth naming outright.
