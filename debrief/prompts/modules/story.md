# Module: story

Record what happened, so the compose stage can tell the story of the flight.

You are not writing the narrative here. You are collecting the beats it will be
built from: the things that happened in this phase, in the order they happened,
with the timestamps that anchor them.

Look for:

- The events that define this phase: the aircraft starting to move, the wheels
  leaving the ground, a turn onto a heading it then holds, arriving somewhere,
  starting down, touching down, stopping.
- What was distinctive about this phase compared with a generic one. What makes
  this flight this flight.
- The mood of it: unhurried, busy, playful, workmanlike.
- Anything a person watching over the pilot's shoulder would ask about.

Keep each claim to one or two sentences and anchor it to a timestamp. Prefer
concrete detail over summary: "the shadow of the aircraft runs alongside on the
grass as it lifts off" beats "a normal takeoff".

Set `interest` to `character` for most of these, and `provenance` honestly. This
module runs on every flight regardless of the camera position, so work with
whatever the viewpoint gives you and say plainly when it gives you little.
