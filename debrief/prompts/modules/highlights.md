# Module: highlights

Find the frames worth going back to.

You are looking for the moments the pilot would pause the video on, or send to
someone else. Not the instructive moments — the good ones.

Look for:

- The best-looking frame in this phase, and what makes it good.
- A moment of unusual light, weather, or terrain.
- The peak of something: the steepest bank, the highest point, the closest pass
  to a feature, the moment the wheels leave the ground.
- A moment that is funny, surprising, or characterful.
- The first and last few seconds of the flight, which often carry more than the
  middle.

Each observation should name the timestamp of the frame itself in
`timestamps[0]`, because that frame will be embedded in the finished debrief.
Say in the `claim` what is in the frame and why it earned a place.

Set `interest` to `character` or `trivia` for most of these. Do not force a
safety angle onto a good picture.

Return at most three per phase. A highlight reel of everything is not a
highlight reel.
