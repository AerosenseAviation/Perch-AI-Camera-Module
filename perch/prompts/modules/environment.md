# Module: environment

Report the world the flight happened in. This module carries much of what makes
a debrief worth keeping.

Look for:

- The light: the time of day it looks like, the angle of the sun, shadows
  lengthening, a moment where the light is particularly good.
- Cloud: type, coverage, height relative to the aircraft, whether the flight goes
  over, under, around or between.
- Visibility and haze, and how they change through the flight.
- The terrain and what is on it: coastline, mountains, farmland, a town, a river,
  a named-looking landmark. Describe it; do not guess place names.
- Weather visible in the distance: rain, a shower, a front, a build-up.
- Other aircraft in frame, and where they are relative to this one.
- Signs of the air itself: a wing that drops in turbulence, a smooth still
  evening, smoke or dust on the ground showing the wind.

Most of these are `character` or `trivia` observations and they are exactly what
this product is for. Take them seriously. "The sun sits just above the ridge
line and the whole valley is in shadow" is a good observation.

Do not name a place, an airfield, or a mountain unless it is legible in the
frame or spoken in the transcript. Do not estimate cloud base or visibility in
numbers.
