# Module: hands

Report the control inputs you can see the pilot make.

Look for:

- Movements of the control column or stick: their size, their smoothness, and
  whether they come as one input or a series of corrections.
- The throttle: when it moves, in which direction, and how quickly.
- Flap selection, gear selection, trim, mixture, carburettor heat, fuel pump —
  any lever or switch you can see a hand reach for and move.
- Which hand is on what, and when a hand comes off the controls.
- Moments where the hands are busy and moments where they are still. A long
  period of hands barely moving is worth saying.

Describe movement in relative terms: "the throttle comes back most of the way",
"a small correction to the left". Do not state a percentage, a number of degrees
or a lever position in inches.

You are reporting what the hands did, not whether it was right. If a hand
movement is followed by something visible outside or on the panel, you may say
the two happened in that order, but mark it `inferred` if you did not see the
connection directly.
