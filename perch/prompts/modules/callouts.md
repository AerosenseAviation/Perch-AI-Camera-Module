# Module: callouts

Report the spoken callouts and checklist work in the transcript.

Look for:

- Checklists read aloud: which one, when, and whether it runs to the end or is
  interrupted.
- Standard callouts at the moments they belong to: airspeed alive, rotate, power
  set, positive rate, runway ahead, and whatever else this pilot uses.
- Callouts made consistently across repeated events, such as several circuits.
- A challenge and response between two people, when there are two people.
- Silence where a callout would usually sit, and busy talk at a quiet moment.
- The pilot narrating to themselves — often the most revealing thing on the tape.

Work from the transcript and cite the nearest frame timestamp. Quote the words
briefly when the wording matters. If the transcript is unclear, say so.

Do not judge the callouts against a published checklist or a syllabus. You do
not have either. Report what was said and when.
