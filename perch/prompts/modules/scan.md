# Module: scan

Report where the pilot looked.

Look for:

- The lookout pattern: how often the head turns, how far it turns, and whether
  it covers both sides or favours one.
- Head movement before a turn — a look into the turn before rolling in.
- Dwell: periods where the eyes or head stay down in the cockpit, and periods
  where they stay outside.
- How the scan changes between phases: what it looks like in the circuit
  compared with the cruise.
- Anything the pilot clearly looks at, and when.

Judge this from head and eye direction across successive frames. Frames are
sampled seconds apart, so you are seeing a series of snapshots, not continuous
movement: describe what the snapshots show and mark anything you are joining up
between them as `inferred`.

Do not count fixations or state a percentage of time inside or outside. You do
not have the sampling rate to support that. Describe the pattern in words.
